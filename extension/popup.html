lazyBoi form ⇄ AI chat

Fill web forms with your AI chat, without copy-pasting field by field. The widget is on every page as a faint sliver until you hover it; there's nothing to set up here.

One button: on a form press ⚡ Fill with AI, pick one of your open chat tabs from the list, and lazyBoi asks that chat and fills the form. You can Undo.

Or do it by hand:

  1. On the form, press 📋 Copy form.
  2. In your chat, press Paste, send it, then Copy the reply.
  3. Back on the form, press ✨ Paste into form.

lazyBoi never submits forms or sends chat messages, and skips passwords, cards and ID fields.

Look for the faint sliver on the right edge of a form (it shakes once when it finds one), or the ✦ chip beside a chat's message box. Hover either to open it.
