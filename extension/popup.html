lazyBoi form ⇄ AI chat

Fill web forms with your AI chat, without copy-pasting field by field. The widget floats on every page; there's nothing to set up here.

  1. On the form: press 📋 Copy form. lazyBoi scans the page and copies a prompt.
  2. In your AI chat (claude.ai, ChatGPT…): press Paste, send it, then press Copy on the reply.
  3. Back on the form: press ✨ Paste into form. It fills straight away, and you can Undo.

lazyBoi never submits forms or sends chat messages, and skips passwords, cards and ID fields.

Drag the dots to move the widget · double-click them to tuck it away
