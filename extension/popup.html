lazyBoi form ⇄ AI chat

Fill web forms with your AI chat, without copy-pasting field by field.

One button: on a form press Fill with AI, pick one of your open chat tabs, and lazyBoi asks that chat and fills the form. You can Undo.

Or do it by hand:

  1. On the form, press Copy form.
  2. In your chat, look for the star icon, press Paste, send it, then Copy the reply.
  3. Back on the form, press Paste into form.

lazyBoi never submits forms or sends chat messages, and skips passwords, cards and ID fields.
